# Design Specification

## Visual Direction

Design a premium futuristic AI SaaS website with a dark cinematic, luxury-tech aesthetic.

The overall interface should feel like an elite AI intelligence command center: sophisticated, minimal, technical, high-end, and enterprise-grade rather than colorful or playful.

## Color & Background

- Deep obsidian / black primary background.
- Layered charcoal-black surfaces for cards and sections.
- Metallic silver and platinum accents.
- White-to-slate metallic gradients for major headings and highlights.
- Restrained luminous white/silver glow.
- Avoid colorful neon gradients.

## Typography

- **Syne** for large headlines and display typography.
- **Inter** for labels, navigation, metrics, and data.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy.
- Large headlines should be bold, geometric, modern, and slightly futuristic.
- Use metallic silver gradient text with a subtle luminous glow.
- Small labels should often be uppercase with generous letter spacing.
- Numerical/data elements can use a compact technical/monospace appearance.

## Atmospheric Effects

Use large, soft, cloud-like ambient radial glows and platinum mist behind major sections.

Use:
- Blurred radial gradients.
- Very subtle atmospheric light.
- Soft depth shadows.
- Faint luminous halos.
- Restrained cloud/mist effects.

The atmosphere should feel premium and cinematic, never flashy.

## Cards & Surfaces

Use glassmorphism and layered dark surfaces.

Cards should have:
- Dark translucent backgrounds.
- Strong backdrop blur.
- Very subtle borders.
- Soft, deep shadows.
- Moderate rounded corners.
- Architectural, slightly sharp geometry rather than overly soft UI.

Create depth by layering translucent surfaces over the dark background.

## Navigation

Use a clean dark translucent header with:
- Compact futuristic logo.
- Uppercase micro-labels.
- Pill-shaped navigation.
- Bright white primary CTA.
- Dark translucent secondary CTA.
- Small status indicators and technical metadata.

The header should feel like a premium AI control interface.

## Layout

Use a wide, spacious desktop-first layout with strong visual hierarchy and generous negative space.

Primary layout language:
- Bento-grid dashboards.
- Large metric cards.
- Supporting information cards.
- Diagnostic panels.
- Technical tables.
- Progress bars.
- Score meters.
- Architecture blocks.
- Analytics modules.

Sections should feel modular, structured, and connected.

## Icons & Graphics

Use minimal Material-style outline or geometric icons.

Icon treatment:
- White or metallic silver.
- Occasional subtle glow.
- Simple and technical.
- No cartoon or illustrative iconography.

Use thin divider lines, HUD-style framing, technical markers, small telemetry indicators, and subtle geometric graphics.

## Buttons

Primary buttons:
- Bright white.
- Dark text.
- Pill-shaped.
- Strong but subtle white glow.
- High contrast.

Secondary buttons:
- Dark translucent surface.
- Light text.
- Subtle border/shadow.
- Pill-shaped.

Interactions should feel smooth and understated.

## Forms & Inputs

Inputs should be:
- Dark and understated.
- Slightly translucent.
- Softly bordered.
- Moderately rounded.
- Clean white typography.
- Minimal focus states with subtle silver illumination.

## Data Visualization

Use monochrome and metallic data visualization.

Preferred tones:
- White.
- Platinum.
- Silver.
- Slate gray.

Use:
- Progress bars.
- Score meters.
- Telemetry indicators.
- Compact charts.
- Data tables.
- Status badges.
- Metric counters.

Avoid bright multicolor dashboards.

## HUD / Technical Language

Throughout the interface, incorporate subtle command-center details:

- Tiny uppercase system labels.
- Telemetry readouts.
- Live-status dots.
- Technical identifiers.
- System states.
- Thin rules and separators.
- Monospace numerical data.
- Compact metadata.
- Small diagnostic markers.

These details should enhance the interface without making it visually cluttered.

## Overall Style

The final visual impression should combine:

**Apple-level minimalism + premium fintech dashboard + futuristic AI command center + brushed platinum / obsidian luxury.**

The design should look expensive, intelligent, precise, calm, and technologically advanced.

## Avoid

Do not use:
- Colorful neon cyberpunk palettes.
- Cartoon graphics.
- Excessive gradients.
- Excessive glass effects.
- Excessive rounded corners.
- Playful startup aesthetics.
- Generic SaaS dashboard styling.
- Visually noisy layouts.
